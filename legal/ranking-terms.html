Ranking rules & terms

Effective 1 September 2026. GadgetsManía is a discovery leaderboard. Paid boosts purchase ranking visibility; they do not purchase reviews, endorsements, clicks, sales or guaranteed traffic.

Live payments are not enabled until the site clearly stops displaying “Test mode”. Test transactions have no monetary value and create no paid advertising entitlement.

1. How ranking works

  • Only successfully completed and webhook-verified Stripe payments count as paid boosts.
  • The daily board counts verified boosts received since 00:00 UTC. The historical board counts the verified cumulative total.
  • Higher verified totals rank first. Editorial picks are clearly marked, always display €0 and are not represented as paid positions.
  • Positions can change immediately when another product receives a higher boost. A payment never guarantees a particular position or duration.

2. Listings

Submitters must provide accurate information and have authority to promote the linked product or act as a genuine supporter. GadgetsManía may correct formatting, suspend or reject misleading, unlawful, unsafe, infringing, adult, gambling, deceptive, malware or non-gadget content.

3. Payments and refunds

Boosts are one-time digital visibility services delivered after successful payment. By requesting immediate publication, the buyer asks performance to begin immediately. Completed boosts are generally non-refundable after publication, except for duplicate charges, technical failure, or a listing rejected before publication. Statutory rights remain unaffected.

4. No endorsement

Rank reflects verified paid momentum, not product quality. Users must perform their own checks before purchasing any linked product. Editorial and affiliate content is identified separately.

5. Moderation and abuse

Automated manipulation, fraudulent payments, impersonation, malicious URLs and attempts to evade moderation are prohibited. GadgetsManía may suspend a listing while investigating disputes or risk signals.

6. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, GadgetsManía is not liable for third-party products, external websites, changing rank, lost expected traffic or indirect losses. Nothing excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

7. Contact

Questions, corrections or refund requests: contact GadgetsManía and include the product URL and Stripe receipt email.
